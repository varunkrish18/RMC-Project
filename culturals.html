Elixora 2026 – Cultural Extravaganza

Rajalakshmi Medical College proudly presented Elixora 2026, a spectacular Cultural Extravaganza held on 21 May 2026. True to its name, Elixora was a magical cultural experience for all faculty and students. The entire MBBS student body and faculty were divided into four teams inspired by the Hogwarts Houses from the Harry Potter series: Slitherians, Hufflepuff, Gryffindor, and Ravenclaw.

The event was organized magnificently with 21 offstage competitions and grand on-stage cultural events. Active participation was also witnessed from Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology, guided by their faculty team. Every event was individually coordinated by a faculty member and a team of student coordinators. The entire movie promotion team of the film "Double Occupancy" entertained the crowd, featuring a thrilling dance jockey event enjoyed by the entire student and faculty community.

Event Highlights:
  • 4 Hogwarts House Theme Teams: Slitherians, Hufflepuff, Gryffindor, Ravenclaw
  • 21 Offstage Competitions & Grand On-stage Cultural Events
  • Overall Winner: Team Slitherians | Runner-Up: Team Ravenclaw
  • Movie Promotion: "Double Occupancy" Film Team Entertainment
  • Dance Jockey Event & Interactive Student-Faculty Participation
  • Participation: Rajalakshmi Medical College & Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology
Date: 21 May 2026 Event Type: Cultural Extravaganza – Inter-House Competition Chief Patron: Dr. Thangam Meghanathan Dean: Dr. Vanitha Krishnaswamy Medical Director: Dr. Sukumaran Annamalai Chief Guests: Mr. Kishen Das (Actor & Singer), Mr. Nejamavey Santhosh, Ms. Reshma Venkatesh, Ms. Samyuktha Viswanathan, Ms. Anandita Sundar (Movie Producer) Participation: MBBS Students, Faculty, Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology
