28 Feb 2026

RESSWORK 1.0 – Rajalakshmi Endoscopic Sinus Surgery Cadaveric Workshop

The Department of ORL, Head & Neck Surgery, in association with the Department of Anatomy at Rajalakshmi Medical College Hospital & Research Institute, organized RESSWORK 1.0 – a CME cum Endoscopic Sinus Surgery Workshop in Otorhinolaryngology. The event aimed to enhance academic understanding and practical surgical skills among faculty, postgraduate students, and practicing ENT clinicians through expert lectures, CT-PNS–based surgical planning, and hands-on cadaveric dissection sessions ranging from basic sinus surgery to advanced lateral skull base approaches.

Key Highlights:
  • 83 Total Participants | TNMC Registered: 82 | Non-TNMC: 1
  • Chief Guest: Padmashri Dr. Mohan Kameswaran – Oration on Evolution of FESS
  • Hands-on Cadaveric Dissection – Basic & Advanced FESS Procedures
  • CT-PNS Interpretation for Surgical Planning
  • Advanced Approaches: Frontal Sinus, Orbital & Lateral Skull Base
  • Prizes Awarded to Top 3 Best Dissectors
Venue: Lecture Hall – I & Anatomy Dissection Hall, RMCH&RI, Pennalur, Sriperumbudur – 602 117 Time: 8:00 AM – 5:30 PM Organising Head: Dr. D. Anand Karthikeyan, MBBS, MS ENT | Professor & HOD, Dept. of ORLHNS Resource Faculty: 4 | Sponsorship: Nil
Explore Event Details
15 May 2026

Breastfeeding Workshop – Department of Paediatrics

A workshop on Breastfeeding was organized by the Department of Paediatrics, RMCH&RI, to create awareness among healthcare professionals regarding the importance of breastfeeding for the newborn and mother. The program emphasized the benefits of exclusive breastfeeding, proper breastfeeding techniques, and the role of healthcare workers in supporting lactating mothers. The workshop aimed to enhance knowledge and promote positive attitudes toward breastfeeding practices for improving maternal and child health outcomes.

Key Highlights:
  • 30 Participants – Doctors & Staff Nurses from Paediatrics & Gynaecology
  • Basics of Breastfeeding – Physiology & Best Practices
  • Hands-on Demonstration: Correct Positioning & Latching Techniques
  • Management of Common Breastfeeding Challenges
  • Exclusive Breastfeeding Awareness – First 6 Months of Life
  • Inaugurated by Dean Dr. K. Vanitha, MD, DCH
Venue: MEU Room, College Block, RMCH&RI Time: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM Organized by: Department of Paediatrics, RMCH&RI Target Audience: Doctors, Staff Nurses – Paediatrics & Gynaecology Departments
