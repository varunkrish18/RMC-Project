Art of Academic Publishing – CME (Hybrid)

A comprehensive CME program organized by the Medical Education Unit (MEU) focused on enhancing research publication skills and academic awareness. The program brought together faculty, postgraduates, research scholars, and undergraduate students across disciplines to explore the nuances of scholarly publishing and journal selection strategies.

Key Highlights:
  • 134 Total Participants | 52 Feedback Respondents
  • NMC Norms for Publication Standards
  • Journal Selection & Predatory Journal Identification
  • Database Training: Scopus, PubMed, Web of Science, Embase
  • ORCID Registration & Article Submission Practices
Organizer: Medical Education Unit (MEU), RMCH&RI Format: Hybrid (Online-Offline) Feedback Rating: 80.8% Excellent | 19.2% Good

12 May 2026

CME – Dermatophytosis Decoded

A comprehensive Continuing Medical Education program addressing the evolving challenges in dermatophytosis management. The event featured expert sessions on epidemiology, clinical features, laboratory diagnosis, antifungal resistance, and cutting-edge treatment approaches.

Key Highlights:
  • Joint Dept. of Pharmacology & Dermatology
  • Expert Panel Discussions
  • E-Poster Presentations
  • AI in Dermatology Session
  • Research Engagement
Venue: RMCH&RI Lecture Hall Target Audience: Faculty, Clinicians, Postgraduates, Students
Explore Event Details
28 Mar 2025

ANNOVATE 2025 – Creative Anatomy Competition

An innovative competition organized by the Department of Anatomy to foster creativity, scientific thinking, and artistic expression among undergraduate medical students. The program provided a unique platform to integrate anatomical knowledge with visual and creative arts, making anatomy learning more engaging and meaningful. The event reflected the institutional vision of holistic medical education.

Competition Categories:
  • Body & Face Painting (9 Teams) – Creative Anatomical Visualization
  • Model Making (15 Teams) – 3D Anatomical & Embryological Models
  • Histo-Rang (10 Teams) – Histology & Microscopic Anatomy
  • Distinguished External Judges from Leading Medical Institutions
  • Certificates & Awards for Winners in Each Category
Total Participants: 34 Teams (34 Competitions) Organizing Chair: Dr. Gunapriya, Professor & Head, Dept. of Anatomy Judges Panel: Professors from Vels, Saveetha, and Rajalakshmi Medical Colleges
Explore Event Details
14 November 2025

CME – Diabetes 2.0: Biochemistry Meets Innovation

A hybrid Continuing Medical Education program organized by the Department of Biochemistry, Annaii Medical College & Hospital (RMCHRI), held on World Diabetes Day. The program bridged basic biochemical sciences with cutting-edge innovations in diabetes management — including AI and technology-driven approaches — and was accredited by the Tamil Nadu Medical Council (TNMC) for 2 Credit Hours. A total of 231 participants attended across online and offline modes.

Key Highlights:
  • TNMC Accredited – 2 Credit Hours | Certificate No. C92809045
  • Biochemical Pathophysiology & Emerging Diagnostic Markers
  • Nutritional, Lifestyle & Smart Living Strategies in Diabetes
  • AI & Tech-Driven Innovations in Diabetes Care
  • Scientific Debate: Plate vs. Physical Activity in Diabetes Control
  • 231 Participants | 6 Expert Speakers from Premier Institutions
Venue: Lecture Hall – I, RMCHRI, Pennalur, Sriperumbudur Target Audience: Faculty, Postgraduates, MBBS Students, Healthcare Professionals Guest Speakers: Chettinad Hospital, Apollo Speciality, Madha Medical College & More
