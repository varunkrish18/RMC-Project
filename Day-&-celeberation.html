  • Expert Speaker: Dr. Sarah Grace Priyadarshini
  • Preventive Healthcare Focus
  • Mental Health & Wellness
  • Reproductive Health Education
  • Interactive Discussion Session

Pongal Celebration 2026

Pongal 2026 was celebrated with great joy and cultural fervor, bringing together students, faculty, and staff to embrace the spirit of gratitude and togetherness. Traditional decorations, cultural performances, and festive activities showcased the rich heritage of Tamil culture while fostering unity within the campus community.

Programme Highlights
  • Traditional Pongal Preparation
  • Cultural Performances and Folk Arts
  • Rangoli and Traditional Attire Competitions
  • Community Gathering and Celebrations
Organized By: Cultural Committee Focus: Tamil Heritage & Cultural Values Outcome: Strengthened Community Spirit and Cultural Appreciation

The Rajalakshmi Anatomy Art Competition was conducted by the Department of Anatomy, RMCH & RI on 6th June 2026. A total of 199 students participated enthusiastically, showcasing their creativity and artistic interpretation of anatomical sciences.

Competition Categories
  • Gross Anatomy
  • Embryology
  • Histology

  • Creative Anatomy
Participants: 199 Students Categories: 5 Competition Divisions Awards: Three Prizes in Each Category

International Yoga Day – Wellness Programme

As part of the institution's wellness initiative, students, faculty, and staff actively participated in the International Yoga Day – Wellness Programme.   • Guided Breathing Exercises and Pranayama
  • Yoga Postures and Asana Practice
  • Mindfulness and Meditation Sessions
  • Stress Management and Emotional Well-being
  • Collective Wellness Pledge by Participants
Organized By: NSS & Student Wellness Cell Focus: Yoga, Meditation & Mindfulness Outcome: Enhanced Physical and Mental Wellness

2025

White Coat Ceremony 2025

The White Coat Ceremony 2025 marked a significant transition for our first-year medical students as they formally entered the noble profession of medicine. The ceremony symbolized their commitment to uphold the values of compassion, ethics, integrity, and patient-centered care. As students donned the white coat for the first time, they took a solemn oath to serve humanity with dedication and professionalism.

Programme Highlights
  • White Coat Investiture Ceremony
  • Professional Oath Taking
  • Inspirational Addresses by Distinguished Guests
  • Commitment to Medical Ethics and Service
Participants: First-Year MBBS Students Focus: Professionalism & Medical Ethics Outcome: Formal Induction into Medical Profession

National Anti-Ragging Day

National Anti-Ragging Day was observed to reinforce the institution's commitment to maintaining a safe, inclusive, and respectful learning environment. Awareness sessions educated students about anti-ragging regulations, ethical conduct, and the importance of mutual respect and responsible behavior on campus.

Programme Highlights
  • Anti-Ragging Awareness Programmes
  • Student Pledge Against Ragging
  • Interactive Discussions and Presentations
  • Promotion of Safe Campus Culture
Organized By: Anti-Ragging Committee Focus: Student Safety & Awareness Outcome: Strengthened Respectful Campus Culture
